--- title: "Scholarometer" excerpt: "Portal and browser extention that enchances Google Scholar profiles by providing a comparison of authors to their colleages in the field." collection: software link: "https://scholarometer.indiana.edu" --- Scholarometer is a social platform to facilitate citation analysis. You provide author field annotations, and our tool uses this crowdsourced data to evaluate the impact of an author's publications. Our impact metric can be compared across fields. Install the browser extension to use Scholarometer!
